Fully automated blastgates (for woodworking/dust collection), including completely non-invasive machine power sensing to automatically open the gates when the machine is turned on.

My most complicated project so far, using SMD rather than all through-hole components, needing to use a hotplate/reflow workflow and having to follow manufacturer data sheets to lay out proper circuits.
